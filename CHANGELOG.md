## [1.0.0]

First release. Everything is KiCad 10 and CERN-OHL-P-2.0.

### Library (`KiCad/`)

- `CSK_PC104_Slot0` and `CSK_PC104_Slot1` footprints: the CubeSat Kit base and
  extended board patterns (outline, H1/H2 headers, mounting holes; keepouts on
  Slot 0), generated from Pumpkin's 810-00253 rev B2 DXFs by `tools/genfp.py`.
- `CSK_PC104_Bus` footprint: the H1/H2 header pads alone, for a second bus
  connector.
- `CSK_Bus` symbol: the 104 bus pins as two units, H1 and H2.
- `SUCHAI_Payload` symbol: the SUCHAI 2 and 3 payload pinout on the same two
  units.

### Templates (`templates/`)

- `CubeSat_PC104_Slot0` and `CubeSat_PC104_Slot1`: KiCad project templates
  with the board outline, J1 on the bus, copper kept 1.27 mm from the edge and
  their own copy of the library.

### Example board (`breakout/`)

- `CSK_Bus_Breakout`: a 4-layer board carrying every bus pin from the stack
  position to a second H1/H2 header pair on the bottom, generated by
  `tools/genbreakout.py`.

### Tooling

- `just check` (CI): library copies, symbol and footprint generators, DRC with
  schematic parity and ERC on every board.
- `just footprints`: regenerate the footprints from the specification's DXFs
  and compare.
- `just fab` and `just dist`: fabrication files and release assets.

### Changed from the repository's earlier, unreleased state

- Relicensed from GPL-3.0 to CERN-OHL-P-2.0. SPEL's Eagle designs and the
  KiCad import of their stack breakout were removed; they remain in the git
  history under GPL-3.0.
